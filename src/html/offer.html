Everything you need to accept and manage payments

OraFi is more than just a payment gateway. It’s a complete payment infrastructure designed to help you accept payments, understand your customers, and manage your business — all in one place.

📊 Track customers & transactions

View all your transactions in one dashboard. Track payment status, amounts, currencies, and customer activity in real time — without building custom tooling.

🔑 API keys for hosted checkout

Generate and manage secure API keys directly from your dashboard. These keys are used when you integrate OraFi into your system using our API or SDK.

Once integrated, you can initiate payments programmatically and redirect customers to OraFi’s hosted checkout — no wallet or blockchain setup required on your side.

🔗 Payment links (no code required)

Create payment links in seconds. Upload the:

  • Amount
  • Title and description
  • Images of what you’re selling

Share the link with your customers and get paid — perfect for creators, merchants, and businesses that want a fast checkout without integration.

💸 Withdraw in crypto or fiat

OraFi gives you flexibility. Withdraw your earnings directly to:

  • Crypto wallets (multi-asset support)
  • Fiat bank accounts (offramping)

Choose what works best for your business — stay on-chain or cash out to fiat when needed.
