Before OraFi, accepting payments in Web3 was far more complicated than it needed to be.

The problem with Web3 payments

For most businesses, accepting blockchain-based payments meant dealing with fragmented tooling, complex wallet integrations, and unreliable payment tracking.

Merchants were forced to:

  • Manually integrate wallets and smart contracts
  • Handle transaction signing and confirmation logic themselves
  • Build custom systems to track payment status
  • Rely on users to complete flows without guarantees
  • Manually reconcile on-chain transactions with off-chain business logic

This created friction for customers, slowed down development teams, and made Web3 payments inaccessible to many legitimate businesses.

How OraFi changes this

OraFi was built to remove this complexity entirely.

Instead of forcing businesses to think in terms of wallets, transactions, and chain-specific logic, OraFi introduces a familiar payment model — one that abstracts blockchain complexity behind clean APIs, hosted checkout, and reliable event notifications.

Payments become predictable. Statuses are clear. Business logic stays off-chain, while settlement happens securely on-chain.

OraFi is multi-asset by design, starting with Sui, and built to scale across chains without forcing merchants to rebuild their systems.

In short, OraFi brings the reliability and simplicity of traditional payment gateways into the Web3 world — without compromising decentralization.
