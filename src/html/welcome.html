OraFi is a decentralized, multi-asset payment infrastructure built for the blockchain ecosystem — similar to Stripe or Paystack in traditional finance.

OraFi enables merchants, developers, and businesses to accept blockchain-based payments across multiple assets, starting with Sui, without worrying about wallet complexity, transaction signing, or blockchain integration.

What you can do with OraFi

✅ Accept payments from multiple blockchain assets
✅ Use hosted checkout pages (no wallet setup required)
✅ Track payment status in real time
✅ Receive reliable webhook notifications
